Subject: [PATCH 0/11] wifi: mt76: mt7615: add MT7668S support

The MT7668S is an SDIO Wi-Fi and Bluetooth combo found in Amlogic TV
boxes. Its Wi-Fi half is an MT7663 sibling whose firmware owns the MAC:
it builds connections through the flat CE commands, answers a smaller
command set, and counts transmit credit in frames.

1-6 are small core changes the part needs, each a no-op for the parts
already supported. 7 adds a per-firmware profile to mt7615, 8 the CE
commands and transmit descriptor in new files, 9 and 10 wire them in, and
11 adds the sdio id.

Tested on an H96 Max M20 (Amlogic S905LTS, meson-gx-mmc, no SDIO
interrupt) against MediaTek's driver on the same access point: joins on
both bands, reconnects in 105-352 ms, and carries about 150 Mbit/s each
way on 5 GHz at 80 MHz and 90 on 2.4 GHz at 20 MHz.

Known limits:
  - the firmware images are not in linux-firmware yet:
    mt7668_patch_e2_hdr.bin, WIFI_RAM_CODE_MT7668.bin,
    WIFI_RAM_CODE2_SDIO_MT7668.bin and EEPROM_MT7668.bin
  - station mode only
  - system suspend is untested
  - remain-on-channel falls back to mac80211's software implementation,
    which cannot leave the operating channel on this firmware
  - DISABLE_KEY is not sent to the firmware, as elsewhere in mt7615
